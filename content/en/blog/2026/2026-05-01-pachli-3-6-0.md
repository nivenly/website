Pachli 3.6.0 has been released, and includes:

- Accessibility options to underline links
- Improved UIs for managing Drafts and scheduled posts
- Better display of posts containing HTML code, quotes, and lists
- Multiple crash fixes

See the [full Pachli 3.6.0 release post](https://pachli.app/pachli/2026/04/29/3.6.0-release.html?utm_medium=referral&utm_source=nivenly.org&utm_campaign=release-3.6.0) for more details on these and other changes.
